# Photos

Put recipe and recipe-type photos here, then point to them in `content.js` under `images`.

- Name files after the dish, lowercase with dashes: `lu-rou-fan.jpg`, `kaya-toast.jpg`.
- Landscape photos work best (they are cropped to 4:3 on cards and 21:9 at the top of a recipe page).
- Keep each file under about 500 KB: 1600 px wide at JPEG quality 80 is plenty.
- A photo that needs a credit (Wikimedia Commons CC BY / CC BY-SA, a friend's photo) goes in as
  `{ src: "assets/images/kaya-toast.jpg", credit: "Photo: Jane Doe, CC BY-SA 4.0", link: "https://..." }`.
